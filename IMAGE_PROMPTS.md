# Cover Image Prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts to generate cover art for each product, tuned to the
Aiselling brand. Works with Midjourney, DALL·E / ChatGPT, Ideogram, Leonardo,
Flux, etc.

## How to use

1. Pick a product below and copy its prompt.
2. Append the **House style** block (so every cover looks like a set).
3. Generate at **16:10** landscape (covers display ~16/10).
4. Upload in **/admin/products → edit product → Cover image**.

**Two tips that matter:**
- **Avoid text in the image** — AI-rendered text usually comes out garbled, and
  your product title already sits next to the cover on the page. These prompts
  ask for *no text*.
- **Keep the palette consistent** — the House style locks in the dark
  background; each product's accent color is in its own prompt.

## House style (append to every prompt)

```
Style: premium SaaS product cover art, abstract 3D render, dark near-black navy
background (deep blue-black), soft volumetric gradient glow, glassmorphism,
subtle floating particles and a faint grid, cinematic studio lighting, smooth
matte and glossy materials, high detail, centered composition with generous
negative space, modern and clean, no text, no words, no logos, no watermark.
Aspect ratio 16:10.
```

(Midjourney users: add `--ar 16:10 --style raw --v 6` at the end instead of the
"Aspect ratio" line.)

## Tweaking

- Too busy? Add: *minimalist, lots of negative space, single focal object*.
- Want it flatter/cheaper to reproduce: swap "abstract 3D render" for *clean flat
  vector illustration, soft gradients*.
- Off-brand color? The accent words (e.g. "electric blue to violet to deep
  purple") are the levers — keep the House style fixed and only change those.
